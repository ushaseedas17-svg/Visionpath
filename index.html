Process

From camera to clarity
in four steps

Continuous Camera Capture

A smartphone camera or smart glasses device streams visual data of the surrounding environment, creating a real-time feed for analysis.

AI Computer Vision Processing

Machine learning models identify and classify objects in milliseconds: people, vehicles, stairs, doors, obstacles, and traffic signals.

Voice Guidance Generation

Text-to-speech converts visual analysis into clear, multilingual audio alerts tailored to user preferences and the current context.

GPS Navigation Integration

Map data combines with visual input to provide contextual directions and route guidance to your chosen destination.
